OUR STORY

Built on the waterfront.
Shaped by our neighbors.

In this imagined community, Cedar Harbor began as a small fishing settlement in 1892. Its working waterfront soon became a gathering place for boat builders, shopkeepers, and families looking for a fresh start.

Today, that same independent spirit lives on in neighborhood cafés, creative studios, family-owned inns, and a thriving makers market. The chamber brings those voices together to support an inclusive, resilient local economy.

All history and statistics on this page are fictional learning-project content.
