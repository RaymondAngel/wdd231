01 / Identity

Site Name

The Business Intelligence Guys identifies the company’s focus on business intelligence. The abbreviation “BIG” creates a memorable brand, while the light-bulb logo represents ideas, insight, and turning data into useful information.

02 / Scope and experience

Site Purpose

The website will introduce the company and explain how its data analytics, dashboard development, and AI consulting services help businesses make informed decisions. Visitors will learn about the company, explore examples of its capabilities, review available services, and contact the company to discuss their needs.

Three Main Pages

Home

The homepage will introduce the company, its mission, and its approach to helping businesses. Brief examples will demonstrate capabilities such as creating dashboards, identifying business trends, and simplifying reporting.

When authentic material is available and permission allows, it may also feature customer testimonials, reviews, or notable mentions in newspaper articles. Article mentions will identify the publication and link to the original source where possible. This section will be omitted if suitable material is unavailable.

Prominent links will invite visitors to explore services or request a consultation.

Services

This page will explain the company’s offerings:

  • Data analytics: Understand patterns, trends, and opportunities in business data.
  • Dashboard development: Present important business measures in clear, interactive displays.
  • AI consulting: Identify practical uses for AI in business workflows.

Each service will include a description, a potential use, and its business benefit. Visitors can continue to Contact Us to discuss a service.

Contact Us

This page will provide company contact information and an inquiry form with fields for name, email address, service of interest, and a message describing the visitor’s needs.

Visible labels, required-field instructions, input checks, and clear feedback will help visitors complete the form. The implementation will accurately explain whether an inquiry was delivered or whether the form is a course-project demonstration.

How the Website Will Work

All three pages will share a consistent header, navigation menu, and footer. Visitors can begin on Home, learn more on Services, and continue to Contact Us when ready to make an inquiry. Every main page will also be accessible directly from the navigation, which will identify the current page.

On small screens, content will stack vertically and navigation will use an expandable menu. On larger screens, navigation will remain visible and related content may appear side by side. Keyboard access, readable text, descriptive links, and meaningful image alternatives will support accessible use.

03 / Visitors

Target Audience and Scenarios

The intended audience is small-business owners, managers, and organizations seeking help understanding their data or improving business processes.

  1. What does The Business Intelligence Guys do, and how can the company help my business?
  2. What examples demonstrate the company’s capabilities?
  3. Which service would help me monitor performance or simplify reporting?
  4. Are there customer reviews or published mentions that I can read?
  5. How can I contact the company to discuss a project?

Roboto is the selected font for the website and this plan, with a generic sans-serif fallback if Roboto is unavailable.

06 / Homepage structure

Written Wireframe Alternative

Under my instructor-approved accommodation, these written descriptions replace visual wireframe sketches. They describe the homepage’s structure, content order, and behavior on small and large screens.

Small-Screen Homepage Layout

The homepage will use a single column in the following reading order:

  1. Header: The company logo and an expandable navigation menu containing Home, Services, and Contact Us. The menu button will announce whether it is expanded or collapsed.
  2. Introduction: A main heading introducing the company’s value, a supporting paragraph, and links labeled “Explore Our Services” and “Contact Us.” Any supporting dashboard image will follow the text.
  3. About the Company: A heading and brief paragraphs explaining the company’s mission, background, and approach.
  4. Capability Examples: Vertically stacked examples of dashboards, data analysis, and AI consulting, each with a short explanation and an accessible supporting image where appropriate.
  5. Reviews and Recognition, When Available: Authentic testimonials, reviews, or newspaper mentions arranged vertically. Article mentions will link to their sources. This section will be omitted when suitable material is unavailable.
  6. Contact Invitation: A brief invitation to discuss a business need, followed by a link to Contact Us.
  7. Footer: Company contact details, navigation links, copyright information, and required project information.

Images and content will fit the screen without horizontal scrolling. Links and controls will have sufficient space for comfortable interaction.

Large-Screen Homepage Layout

The homepage will use a centered content area with consistent spacing:

  1. Header: The company logo on the left and visible Home, Services, and Contact Us navigation links on the right.
  2. Introduction: The main heading, introductory text, and action links beside a relevant dashboard or business-data image.
  3. About the Company: A clearly labeled section explaining the company’s mission, background, and approach.
  4. Capability Examples: Three examples in a row so visitors can compare data analytics, dashboards, and AI consulting capabilities.
  5. Reviews and Recognition, When Available: Clearly separated testimonial or review blocks and newspaper mentions identifying the publication and linking to the article.
  6. Contact Invitation: A section spanning the content area that encourages visitors to contact the company.
  7. Footer: Contact information, navigation, and project information arranged in readable columns.

Both layouts will present the same essential information in a consistent reading order. Columns will become a single stack as available space decreases.
